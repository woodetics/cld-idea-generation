Three things to remember

Max is for your own work

Use your login to do work and build things. Anything that serves other people 24/7 runs on an API key, and Max includes $100 a month of API credit for that.

Usage doesn't roll over

Queue work with Routines and overnight builds so Claude keeps going while you sleep. An idle week is money burned.

You are the editor

Agents are great at doing work and bad at judging it. Keep a human check on anything a client sees.
