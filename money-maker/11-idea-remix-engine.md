# Idea Remix Engine

How to generate your own original ideas from other people's ideas, forever. The rule: never copy a full idea. Steal the BRICKS, then rebuild.

---

## The formula

```
[A proven DEMAND signal] + [a cheap SUPPLY trick] + [an underserved AUDIENCE] = a new offer
```

Examples from this folder:
- Demand: "AI receptionists resell at $400 to $800" + Supply: AI voice agents at $0.07/min + Audience: immigrant-owned businesses -> G7 Bilingual AI Receptionist.
- Demand: "podcasters pay $700/month for clips" + Supply: Whisper + FFmpeg + Claude picks moments + Audience: churches -> G13 Church Clipping.
- Demand: "SaaS needs marketing" + Supply: Remotion templates + Audience: changelog pages nobody reads -> G6 Changelog Videos.

## The 7 remix moves

1. Swap the AUDIENCE. Take a service that works for one industry and move it to an underserved one (dentists -> veterinarians -> funeral homes -> churches -> trade schools).
2. Swap the LANGUAGE. Take a service that works in English and run it in Spanish, German, Portuguese, Tagalog (and sell it to diaspora businesses in the US/UK/AU).
3. Swap the MODEL. Service -> product (skill pack), product -> service (done-for-you), one-time -> subscription, fee -> revenue share, retainer -> pay-per-result.
4. Invert the TARGET. Everyone targets "no website" businesses, target "DEAD website" businesses. Everyone sells to creators, sell to creators' EDITORS.
5. Stack TWO data sources. Permits + Google reviews. Filings + LinkedIn jobs. Reddit pains + Amazon reviews.
6. Move UPSTREAM. Instead of clipping, become the person who runs clipping campaigns (G24). Instead of using a tool, sell the setup of the tool.
7. Find the "boring tax". Any task a business does weekly, hates, and is easy to automate = a subscription.

## Random combo generator (do this weekly)

Pick one from each list, force an idea in 5 minutes, write it down even if it's dumb:

```
Audience: ________   Supply trick: ________   Demand signal: ________   Model: ________
Idea: ______________________________________________
Who would pay first? ________   How would I find 20 of them? ________
Could I make a free demo in under 2 hours? Y / N
```

Example spin: Funeral homes + Remotion + "tribute videos" + per-video fee = memorial slideshow videos with music and captions generated from family photos, $99 to $199 each, sold through the funeral home (they take a cut). That's G25 now, free idea.

## Validation in 48 hours (before building anything big)

1. Write the offer in 3 sentences.
2. Make ONE free demo for a real target.
3. Send it to 20 real targets.
4. 0 replies -> change the audience or the message, not the whole idea. 0 replies after 100 -> kill it.
5. 1 "how much?" -> you have something.

## Signals to watch (use Agent-Reach on your own machine)

- Reddit: "is there a tool that...", "I'd pay for...", "how do I automate..."
- GitHub issues: "hosted version?", "can someone set this up?"
- Upwork: job categories with rising post counts.
- YouTube: tutorials on a tool getting lots of views but poor comments ("still confused") = setup service demand.
- Xiaohongshu/Bilibili: products/formats trending there but not yet on TikTok US.
- Fiverr: search volume hints (categories with few sellers but many buyer requests).
