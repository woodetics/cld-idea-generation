## Getting paid in USD from outside the US

- Wise: USD account details, no receiving fee (Wise's own claim), conversion spread about 0.4 to 0.6% above mid-market. Use as your main account.
- Payoneer: $29.95 annual fee if you receive under $6,000/year, up to 1% receiving fee, about $1.50 withdrawal to a PH bank (2026 guide). Keep it for Fiverr (smoother integration) and platforms that need it.
- PayPal: widely accepted by clients, but worse conversion rates. Withdraw to Wise or your bank only when needed, or convert via Wise.
- Upwork/Fiverr: built-in payouts to Wise/Payoneer/PayPal.

## Selling digital products without a US company

- You likely can't use Stripe directly (Stripe isn't available in many Asian countries). Options:
  - Merchant of Record platforms: Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Paddle, Gumroad, Payhip. They sell the product legally, handle VAT/sales tax, and pay you out. Check each one's supported payout countries yourself.
  - Stripe Atlas (US LLC) costs money upfront, only worth it once you're making real revenue.
- Substack / beehiiv paid newsletters for subscriptions (check payout support for your country).

## Invoicing clients directly

- Wise invoices / payment links, PayPal invoices, or Payoneer payment requests.
- Always invoice in USD/GBP/EUR/AUD. Never your local currency.
- Terms: 50% upfront on projects, monthly retainers billed in advance.

## Looking "local" to Western clients (honestly)

- Work some hours overlapping their time zone.
- A clean business email on your own domain.
- A simple site with your work, testimonials and a booking link (Cal.com free).
- "I work remotely with clients in the US, UK and Australia" is honest and works.

## Taxes

- Declare income where you live. Keep a simple spreadsheet of every payment (date, client, amount, currency, fees). Platforms send income summaries, so it will show up anyway.
