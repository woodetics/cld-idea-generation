# Start Here: The Broke Playbook (first 30 days)

Assumptions: you're a non-coder, you have under $50, you need money soon, and you'll sell to US / UK / EU / AU / CA buyers because that's where the exchange rate works for you.

The big idea: run ONE fast-cash track and ONE compounding track at the same time. Fast cash pays rent. Compounding builds the thing that pays you while you sleep. Don't start five things.

## Day 1 (today)

1. Open a Wise account (USD receiving details). Also Payoneer if you plan to use Fiverr. Takes a few days to verify, so start NOW.
2. Create a Fiverr seller profile and an Upwork profile. Don't post gigs yet, just get verified.
3. Apply to Mercor, Outlier and DataAnnotation (check which accept your country). This is your cash floor. Approval can take days to weeks, so apply on day 1.
4. Pick your fast-cash track from the menu below.
5. Read `06-hidden-gems.md` and pick ONE compounding track.

## Fast-cash menu (pick ONE)

### Track A: Claude Code setup gigs (fastest if you're comfortable talking to people)
- Fiverr "Claude Code" searches reportedly up 938% YoY. Most buyers are non-technical business owners who heard about it and are scared of the terminal.
- Gig: "I will set up Claude Code / Claude Cowork for your business with custom skills". $75 basic (install + 1 skill), $200 standard (3 custom skills + walkthrough video), $450 premium (5 skills + 1 automation + 30-day support).
- You learn by doing. Every gig = a new skill you can resell (see `08`).
- Full details: `02-claude-code-agent-services.md` S1. Copy: `09-outreach-scripts-and-pricing.md`.

### Track B: Website Rescue (fastest if you can do cold outreach)
- Find businesses with 20+ Google reviews whose website is DEAD (expired domain, parked page, broken SSL, 404). They already believed in having a site, so they're easier to sell than "no website" businesses.
- Claude Code rebuilds a modern one-page site in an hour. Host the DEMO free on Vercel/Netlify. Send them the live link.
- Charge $300 to $600 setup + $29 to $49/month hosting + edits.
- Full details: `06-hidden-gems.md` G1.

### Track C: Clipping (fastest if you hate talking to people)
- Whop Content Rewards: about $1 per 1,000 views median, some campaigns pay $3 to $6.
- No client, no sales call. You pick a campaign, cut clips from the provided content, post on TikTok / Reels / Shorts, submit.
- Weakness: unpredictable, payouts held, possible view rejections. Use it as a skill builder and portfolio, then sell clipping retainers to podcasters ($700/month for 15 clips is a published benchmark).
- Full details: `03-video-and-shorts.md` V3 and V4.

## Week 1

- Day 1 to 2: set up accounts, pick tracks.
- Day 2 to 3: build your PROOF. For Track A, record a 3-minute screen video of Claude Code building something (a landing page, a skill). For Track B, build 3 demo rescue sites for real businesses. For Track C, post 5 clips.
- Day 3 to 7: outreach volume. Track A: post the gig, send 10 Upwork proposals per day (with a demo, see `09`). Track B: 20 personalized emails per day with the live demo link. Track C: 3 to 5 clips per day.

## Week 2

- Look at what got replies. Double down on the channel that got ANY response. Kill the one that got zero after 100 attempts.
- First paid job: overdeliver, ask for a review/testimonial, ask "who else do you know who needs this?"

## Week 3 to 4

- Turn the first 1 to 3 jobs into a template. Every repeated task becomes a Claude skill (this is the whole game: your skills folder becomes your factory).
- Raise prices after 3 reviews.
- Start the compounding track if you haven't.

---

## Rules that keep you alive

1. Volume beats cleverness in outreach. 100 targeted messages before judging an idea.
2. Never pay for a course. Everything in those courses is in this folder or free on YouTube.
3. Charge in USD/GBP/EUR/AUD. Never quote in your local currency.
4. Proof over promises. A live demo link beats any pitch.
5. Keep 1 client happy before chasing 5.
6. Keep a "wins" file of screenshots of results. That's your portfolio.
7. Don't buy "done-for-you" automation systems or "AI agency in a box" offers. They sell to broke people.
