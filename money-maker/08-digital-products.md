# Digital Products

Products scale, services pay faster. Rule: build products OUT OF your service work. Every client problem you solve twice becomes a product.

---

## P1. Claude Skills and Skill Packs

- Proof: Ryan Doser reports $5,000+ in about 50 days selling a Claude skills product at $99, plus a $1,000 consulting upsell.
- Marketplaces and cuts:
  - Claude Market (claudemarket.ai): 10% fee on sales.
  - MCP Market (mcpmarket.com/sell): 85% to you via Stripe Connect (check Stripe Connect availability in your country).
  - AgentAlley: 70% of net.
  - Agensi (25,500+ skills listed).
  - Anthropic's Claude Marketplace (opened Sept 2026, plugin submissions open to paid-plan devs): free distribution, no evidence of paid sales. Use for visibility.
  - Your own: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Payhip (Merchants of Record handle tax).
- Pricing: one-off for self-contained skills, subscription if it needs updates.
- What sells: profession-specific packs (see G20), not generic "write better emails" skills.
- Product ideas:
  - Property manager pack, recruiter pack, insurance agent pack, mortgage broker pack, bookkeeper pack, real estate agent pack, YouTube creator pack (scripts, titles, thumbnails briefs, clip finder), Etsy seller pack, Shopify store pack, podcast producer pack, church media pack, teacher pack.
  - "Cold outreach in your voice" pack (voice profiling skill + email + DM + follow-up skills).
  - "Remotion video templates" pack for creators.
- Funnel: 1 free skill as a lead magnet -> paid pack -> setup call upsell -> custom skills service.

## P2. MCP Servers

- Hosted/marketplace MCP servers that succeed reportedly earn $500 to $3,000/month per platform (unverified).
- Raw MCP access sells when it wraps a paid API or a sensitive enterprise workflow (SLA, audit logs).
- Ideas: connectors for niche industry software with no Claude connector, public-data MCPs (permits, filings, grants) as a subscription, "Agent-Reach style" niche data readers.

## P3. n8n / Make Templates

- Price: $29 simple to $299+ complex industry-specific.
- Claimed: $500 to $1,500/month after 3 to 4 months with 10 to 15 templates (vendor claim).
- Generic templates get copied everywhere. Industry-specific wins (e.g., "Dental clinic recall + review request automation", "Real estate lead -> SMS -> CRM -> follow-up sequence", "Podcast episode -> clips -> social scheduling").
- n8n affiliate reportedly 30% of net for 12 months (verify).
- Sell on Gumroad, own site, n8n community, Make marketplace.

## P4. Notion / Google Sheets / Airtable Templates

- Etsy allows AI-assisted digital downloads IF you play a clear creative role and disclose AI use (Oct 22, 2026 update).
- Etsy BANS: AI prompt bundles as standalone products, AI-created "functional guides" (patterns, recipes, instructions), bot-made listings, mass automated listing generation.
- Templates: not clearly addressed. A mainly human-designed spreadsheet tool is likely OK, but verify Etsy's Creativity Standards. Gumroad/Payhip/Lemon Squeezy have fewer restrictions.
- What sells: business tools with formulas that SAVE time (quote calculators, inventory trackers, client CRMs for a niche, YouTube channel planners with RPM calculators, freelancer invoice + tax trackers per country, wedding vendor trackers).
- Claude Code edge: it can build actually-functional Google Sheets with Apps Script automation, which most template sellers can't.

## P5. Datasets and Lead Lists

- Weekly new-business filings (G4), building permits (G5), grants/RFPs (G14), "remote AI jobs open to non-US" (G16), China trend reports (G10), Reddit pain-point databases (G11).
- Subscriptions beat one-time sales.
- Sell on: Gumroad memberships, Substack/beehiiv paid tiers, your own site with Lemon Squeezy.

## P6. Remotion Video Templates

- Sell parameterized video templates (change a JSON, get a new video): stat videos, podcast audiograms, listing videos, quote reels, changelog videos.
- Buyers: developers and agencies who use Remotion, creators who want a "fill in and render" tool.
- Price: $29 to $149 per template, $199 to $399 bundles.

## P7. Micro-SaaS (once you have a service that repeats)

- Typical price $5 to $15/month (generic); niche B2B tools can charge $29 to $99.
- First milestone: 10 paying customers. Find them in niche subreddits/Discords, give free accounts for feedback.
- Best micro-SaaS ideas come from your service work: the review reply desk (S4) as self-serve, the competitor review gap report (G3) as self-serve, the permit leads (G5) as a dashboard.
- Most vibe-coded SaaS make nothing at first. Don't START here. Graduate to it.

## P8. Mini-Courses / Paid Guides (careful)

- "How I got my first 5 clients with Claude Code as a non-coder" type guides sell once you have REAL results to show. Sell AFTER, not before.
- Price: $27 to $97.
- Avoid: selling get-rich courses with fake results. It's the most saturated market and buyers are burned.
