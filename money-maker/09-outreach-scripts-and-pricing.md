# Outreach Scripts and Pricing

These drafts are written in your voice (from your writing-style profile): plain spoken, short paragraphs, honest about gaps, proof instead of claims, one word in caps for emphasis, parenthetical asides, no dashes, no corporate fluff. Swap the [brackets].

Cold outreach rules (US CAN-SPAM / UK PECR): real business address in the footer, a working unsubscribe line, honor opt-outs, no fake "Re:" subjects.

```
It's mobile friendly, has your hours, services and reviews, and a button that calls you directly. If you like it, I can put it on your domain and keep it running for you. If not, no worries at all, it's yours to look at either way.

Setup is $[399] and then $[39]/month for hosting and any changes you need.

Anyway, I just thought you should know people are landing on a dead page right now.

[Your name]
[Your business name] · [address]
Not interested? Just reply "no" and I won't email again.
```
