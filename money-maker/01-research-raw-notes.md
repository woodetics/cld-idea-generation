## 3. YouTube "inauthentic content" policy (the faceless channel trap)

- July 15, 2025: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content", clarifying it covers mass-produced and repetitive videos.
- The rule targets MASS-PRODUCED, LOW-EFFORT content, not AI or faceless formats per se. Same standard as human-made content.
- Faceless is not a disqualifying category. But channels lacking a distinct voice, personality or creative direction may struggle.
- January 2026 enforcement wave: 16 channels removed from the Partner Program, one reportedly with 4.7 billion views and $10 million/year revenue. Removed for mass-produced content, not for using AI tools. [several blogs agree]
- Reported July 2026 update split inauthentic content into 3 types: generic/repetitive, unsatisfying/off-putting, and AI personas on sensitive topics. [SINGLE, unverified against YouTube's own text]
- RED FLAGS cited: AI voiceover over stock footage with no commentary, slideshows without narrative, compilations without transformation, high-volume template uploads, AI "expert" personas on health/finance/legal topics.
- Takeaway for us: if you run faceless, every video needs a real angle, original script, transformation, a consistent persona or point of view. Volume alone gets you demonetized.

## 5. Whop "Content Rewards" clipping (paid per view)

- Typical rate about $1 per 1,000 views. Range about $0.20 to $6.
- Analysis of 708 live campaigns (mid-October 2026): half pay $1 or less per 1k, only 12% pay $3+.
- Sample of 100 campaigns on Oct 12, 2026: $0.20 to $5.00 per 1k, median $1.30.
- Examples: Roobet paid $1.50 per 1k with a $250k budget. MUTUUM paid $6 per 1k. (Note: gambling and crypto brands pay the highest, which is its own gray area.)
- Typical per-clip earnings cap about $200.
- Content Rewards takes 10% of per-view payouts.
- Worked example: 100,000 views at $1/1k = $100, minus 10% = $90, minus Whop payout fee.
- Payout rounds: 7 days of earning + 3-day hold, so about every 10 days.
- Complaints: post-view rejections, bot-flag disputes, earnings holds up to 90 days.
- Pay is per view, not follower count. New accounts can earn if clips hit.
